Mathematics is the study of numbers, quantities, shapes, and patterns. Basic arithmetic operations include addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division. 

Algebra introduces variables and equations, allowing us to solve problems with unknown quantities. Geometry deals with shapes, sizes, and properties of space.

Calculus is the mathematical study of continuous change, with two main branches: differential calculus and integral calculus. Statistics involves collecting, analyzing, and interpreting data.

Key mathematical concepts include functions, graphs, derivatives, integrals, probability, and matrices. Mathematics is fundamental to science, engineering, economics, and many other fields.
